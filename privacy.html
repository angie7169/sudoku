Your data lives only on your device. No account · No tracking · No servers · No ads.

1. 我们收集的数据

1. Data We Collect

个人数据：无。数独挑战不需要注册账号、邮箱或登录，我们不询问你的姓名、电话、位置等信息。

Personal data: none. Sudoku Challenge needs no account, email address, or sign-in, and we never ask for your name, phone number, or location.

游戏数据：你的对局进度与断点、每日挑战记录、金币与皮肤、任务与成就、棋力与统计、个人设置，全部只保存在你设备上应用的本机存储中。这些数据是技术性游戏数据，与你的身份无关。

Game data: your progress and saves, daily-challenge records, coins and themes, missions and achievements, Rating and statistics, and personal settings all live only in the app’s local storage on your device. This is technical game data, unrelated to your identity.

使用数据与设备标识：我们不收集任何分析统计、崩溃上报、设备标识符（IDFA/IDFV）或行为追踪数据，也不跨应用、跨网站追踪你。应用内的行为日志仅写入本机系统日志，用于开发调试，不会离开你的设备。

Usage data & device identifiers: we collect no analytics, crash reports, device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), or behavioral-tracking data, and we do not track you across apps or websites. In-app event logs are written only to the device’s local system log for development debugging and never leave your device.

2. 数据的使用方式

2. How We Use Data

你的游戏数据仅用于在你自己的屏幕上展示进度、结算评分与保存设置，一切计算都在本机完成。任何数据都不会被上传、共享、出售或在别处处理。我们不使用你的数据进行广告、画像或分析。

Your game data is used only to show progress, scores, and settings on your own screen — every computation happens on-device. Nothing is uploaded, shared, sold, or processed elsewhere. We never use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

3. 数据的存储与保留

3. Storage & Retention

所有数据保存在应用的本机存储（系统应用数据空间）中，直到你删除它为止：可以在应用内「我的 → 账号与数据 → 清除本地数据」主动清空，或直接卸载应用——卸载后全部数据被永久删除，不会在任何地方留存副本。没有云端备份，数据也不会在设备间迁移。

All data stays in the app’s on-device storage (the system app-data container) until you delete it: clear it yourself under Me → Account & Data → Clear Local Data, or simply uninstall — uninstalling permanently deletes everything with no copies anywhere. There is no cloud backup, and data never moves between devices.

4. 第三方服务

4. Third-Party Services

当前版本没有接入任何第三方数据服务：无广告网络（不含 AdSupport / SKAdNetwork）、无统计分析 SDK、无追踪器、无社交 SDK，也没有内购。

The current version integrates no third-party data services: no ad networks (no AdSupport / SKAdNetwork), no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no social SDKs, and no in-app purchases.

系统权限方面：应用仅在你主动使用「保存分享成绩卡到相册」功能时向相册写入图片；我们不读取你的相册内容，也不会上传。未来版本若提供内购、广告或云同步等能力，我们将更新本协议并明确告知所涉及的数据处理方式。

System permissions: the app writes an image to your photo library only when you deliberately save a share card; it never reads your library or uploads anything. If purchases, ads, or cloud sync arrive in a future version, we will update this policy and clearly explain the data handling involved.

5. 你的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，隐私法规定的大多数权利（访问、更正、删除、可携带）已经天然满足——我们没有任何关于你的可披露或可删除的信息。设备上的数据你拥有完全控制权，可随时删除。加州（CCPA）与欧盟/英国（GDPR）居民如有疑问也可直接联系我们。我们不「出售」CCPA 所定义的个人信息。

Because we collect no personal data, most rights under privacy law (access, correction, deletion, portability) are already satisfied by design — we hold nothing about you to disclose or delete. You have full control over on-device data and can delete it anytime. California (CCPA) and EU/UK (GDPR) residents are welcome to contact us with questions. We do not “sell” personal information as defined by the CCPA.

6. 儿童隐私

6. Children’s Privacy

数独挑战是一款全年龄休闲益智游戏，无需注册、不收集任何个人信息，适合儿童使用。监护人如需清除孩子的游戏数据，直接卸载应用或在应用内清除即可。

Sudoku Challenge is an all-ages casual puzzle game: no sign-up, no personal-data collection, and it is suitable for children. Guardians can clear a child’s game data by uninstalling the app or using the in-app reset.

7. 国际用户

7. International Users

应用面向全球提供。由于没有任何数据离开你的设备，我们不存在跨境数据传输。

The app is available worldwide. Since no data ever leaves your device, we make no cross-border data transfers.

8. 数据安全

8. Data Security

你设备上的数据受 iOS 内置的数据保护机制（文件级加密等）保护。我们不在任何外部系统上传输或存储你的数据。

Data on your device is protected by iOS’s built-in data protection (such as file-level encryption). We never transmit or store your data on any external system.

9. 协议变更

9. Changes to This Policy

我们可能不时更新本隐私协议。重大变更将反映在页面顶部的「最后更新」日期中，并在应用内同步展示新版本。

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and the new version will appear in the app as well.

10. 联系方式

10. Contact

对隐私有疑问或提出请求？联系 sudoku@12533.uk，我们力争在 30 天内回复。

Questions or privacy requests? Contact sudoku@12533.uk — we aim to reply within 30 days.
